## Purpose

Map a natural-language observability job offline to the owning OCI O&M service, its owning skill and its registered official documentation.

## Users

Assistants choosing an OCI O&M service offline, and maintainers who keep the catalog current.

## Scope

- catalog schema and the 12 required service ids
- ranking and ordering rules
- catalog_query CLI contract (--top, --json, --list, --validate)
- router skill content

## Non-goals

- skill reachability and overlap (PRD-017)
- live URL reachability (PRD-016)
- pricing (the catalog stores a pointer note only)

## Source anchors

- PLAN.md §3 item 1 and job-to-service table; §4 catalog; §5 catalog_query (6628620, 7bcd39f)
- catalog/services.json (12 services, 27 doc entries, checkedAt 2026-10-09) and services.schema.json (identical at both revisions)
